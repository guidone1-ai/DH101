# Accessibility Statement

> Commitment to universal access, semantic design, and inclusive digital media.

## Design Considerations in This Archive
Accessibility is an essential ethical component of digital humanities scholarship. In constructing this portfolio and archive, the following measures have been implemented:

1. **High Contrast Typography:** The black-and-white noir aesthetic relies on deep obsidian backgrounds and crisp, high-contrast text (`#ffffff` and `#ededed`) satisfying WCAG AAA contrast ratio standards.
2. **Semantic HTML5:** Native landmark elements (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<aside>`, `<footer>`) are employed throughout to facilitate screen reader navigation.
3. **Keyboard & Screen Reader Navigation:** All interactive elements, including track selectors, the resume modal, and external links, are fully accessible via keyboard (`Tab`, `Enter`, `Escape`).
4. **Descriptive Alternative Text:** All embedded images and figures in studio makes and reflections will include comprehensive alt text describing both visual content and analytical relevance.
5. **Print Styles:** The resume features an automated print stylesheet stripping dark backgrounds for clean, accessible physical or PDF reading.

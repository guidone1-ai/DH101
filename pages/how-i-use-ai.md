# How I Use AI

> Critical reflection on machine participation, algorithmic tools, and human authorship.

## Ethos of AI Use
In this course, I approach Artificial Intelligence as a tool for critical inquiry, prototyping, and ideation rather than as an uncritical replacement for human intellectual labor.

When generative AI systems are used in this portfolio:
1. **Critical Discernment:** Every AI suggestion is actively scrutinized, modified, or rejected based on my own critical and conceptual goals.
2. **Transparent Attribution:** Any AI contribution—whether for brainstorming, code structuring, or textual revision—is documented using the course [AI Use Log](../ai-log/template-ai-log.md).
3. **Ethical Scrutiny:** I actively consider the environmental impact, training data extraction practices, and labor issues embedded within large commercial models.

## Workflow & Documentation
- **Prompt Iteration:** I track initial prompts and subsequent refinements to study model behavior and biases.
- **Human Authorship:** The conceptual core, analytical arguments, and creative decisions remain distinctly mine.

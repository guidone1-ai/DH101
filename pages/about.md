# About Eva Guidone

> Student, critical maker, and researcher in Digital Humanities at Denison University.

## Introduction
Welcome to my DH101 digital archive. I am an undergraduate student exploring how computation, culture, and creative labor intersect. 

My work in this course investigates the tangible and ethical dimensions of technology: reverse-engineering everyday systems, questioning algorithmic governance, and experimenting with intentional, responsible forms of digital making.

## Interests & Focus Areas
- **Critical Making & Physical Computing:** Moving beyond digital abstraction to touch, deconstruct, and understand physical and computational interfaces.
- **Algorithmic Culture & Ethics:** Analyzing the impacts of generative models, corporate platforms, and data infrastructures on society and artistic expression.
- **The Noir / R&B Aesthetic:** Framing digital scholarship through musical and editorial metaphors—approaching weekly projects as tracks, liner notes, and vinyl archives.
- **Inclusive Digital Design:** Building web spaces prioritizing accessibility, low-carbon efficiency, and open scholarly communication.
